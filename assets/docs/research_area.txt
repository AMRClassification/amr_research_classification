## **1. Research Area Categories**

### **1.1 Basic Research**

#### **2100 Research Area / Basic Research / Fundamental**
- **Focus:** Investigating fundamental biological processes without immediate application toward a specific product.
- **Includes:**
  - Studies on pathogen mechanisms, transmission, virulence.
  - Fundamental immunology, genetics, and microbiome research.
  - One Health interactions involving humans, animals, and the environment.
- **Technology Readiness Levels (TRL):** 1-2
- **Keywords:** pathogen mechanisms, virulence, immunology, genetics, microbiome, One Health

#### **2200 Research Area / Basic Research / Towards a Product**
- **Focus:** Translational research aimed at applying fundamental discoveries to develop new products.
- **Includes:**
  - Development of models and technologies for drug and vaccine design.
  - Early-stage identification of potential therapeutic targets.
- **Technology Readiness Levels (TRL):** 2-3
- **Keywords:** translational research, drug design, vaccine design, therapeutic targets, early-stage

### **1.2 Therapeutics**

#### **3100 Research Area / Therapeutics / Discovery**
- **Focus:** Early-stage research to identify and validate new therapeutic targets or active compounds.
- **Includes:**
  - Target validation, hit identification, hit-to-lead, and lead optimization.
  - Screening of compounds for biological activity against specific pathogens.
- **TRL:** 4-5 (aligned with Discovery Phase)
- **Keywords:** target validation, hit identification, lead optimization, compound screening

#### **3200 Research Area / Therapeutics / Development**
- **Focus:** Progressing identified therapeutic candidates through preclinical and clinical development toward commercialization.
- **Includes:**
  - Preclinical studies assessing efficacy and safety.
  - Clinical trials (Phase 1-3) to evaluate safety, dosage, and efficacy in humans.
  - Reformulation, repurposing, and manufacturing process validation.
- **TRL:** 6-8 (aligned with Development Phase)
- **Subcategories:**
  - **3201:** Phase 1 Clinical Trials
  - **3202:** Phase 2 Clinical Trials
  - **3203:** Phase 3 Clinical Trials
  - **3204:** Approval Pipeline
  - **3205:** Marketed Products Pipeline
- **Keywords:** clinical trials, Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, preclinical, efficacy, safety, reformulation

#### **3400 Research Area / Therapeutics / Approval & Post approval**
- **Focus:** Activities related to regulatory approval and post-market surveillance of therapeutic products.
- **Includes:**
  - Submission of regulatory dossiers (e.g., NDA, NADA).
  - Post-approval requirements such as pharmacovigilance and product optimization.
  - Expansion of product labels and additional indications.
- **TRL:** 9 (aligned with Approval and Post-Approval Phase)
- **Keywords:** regulatory submission, NDA, NADA, pharmacovigilance, post-market surveillance

### **1.3 Vaccines**

#### **4110 Research Area / Vaccines / Discovery**
- **Focus:** Early-stage vaccine research focused on identifying and characterizing vaccine components.
- **Includes:**
  - Antigen identification, adjuvant selection, and carrier/delivery system development.
  - Immunogenicity testing using serum samples from exposed individuals.
- **TRL:** 4-5
- **Keywords:** antigen identification, adjuvant selection, delivery system, immunogenicity

#### **4120 Research Area / Vaccines / Development**
- **Focus:** Progressing vaccine candidates through preclinical and clinical development stages.
- **Includes:**
  - Preclinical safety and efficacy studies.
  - Clinical trials (Phase 1-3) to assess safety, immunogenicity, and efficacy.
- **TRL:** 6-8
- **Subcategories:**
  - **4122:** Phase 1 Clinical Trials
  - **4123:** Phase 2 Clinical Trials
  - **4124:** Phase 3 Clinical Trials
- **Keywords:** clinical trials, Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, preclinical, safety, efficacy

#### **4140 Research Area / Vaccines / Approval & Post approval**
- **Focus:** Regulatory approval and post-market activities for vaccines.
- **Includes:**
  - Submission of vaccine dossiers for regulatory approval.
  - Post-approval monitoring and additional research for optimization.
- **TRL:** 9
- **Keywords:** regulatory submission, approval, post-market monitoring, optimization

### **1.4 Preventives - Other**

#### **4200 Research Area / Preventives Other / Other_PreventivesOther**
- **Focus:** Non-vaccine products that prevent diseases without inducing specific immune memory.
- **Includes:**
  - Prophylactic treatments, immune modulators, and breeding for disease resistance.
  - Development of anti-biofilm agents and other preventive technologies.
- **TRL:** Varies based on specific preventive strategy.
- **Keywords:** prophylactic treatments, immune modulators, disease resistance, anti-biofilm

#### **4210 Research Area / Preventives Other / Discovery**
- **Focus:** Early-stage research identifying new preventive strategies and agents.
- **Includes:**
  - Identification of new immune modulators or prophylactic compounds.
  - Genetic studies for breeding disease-resistant organisms.
- **TRL:** 1-3
- **Keywords:** immune modulators, prophylactic compounds, genetic studies, disease resistance

#### **4220 Research Area / Preventives Other / Development**
- **Focus:** Advancing preventive agents from discovery to preclinical and clinical stages.
- **Includes:**
  - Preclinical testing of prophylactic agents.
  - Clinical trials for immune modulators or other preventive products.
- **TRL:** 4-8
- **Keywords:** preclinical testing, clinical trials, prophylactic agents, immune modulators

#### **4240 Research Area / Preventives Other / Approval & Post approval**
- **Focus:** Regulatory approval and post-market activities for preventive products.
- **Includes:**
  - Submission of dossiers for approval of preventive agents.
  - Post-market surveillance and optimization studies.
- **TRL:** 9
- **Keywords:** regulatory submission, approval, post-market surveillance, optimization

### **1.5 Diagnostics**

#### **5100 Research Area / Diagnostics / Discovery**
- **Focus:** Early-stage research to identify new diagnostic targets and develop prototype diagnostic tools.
- **Includes:**
  - Identification of causative agents and resistance profiles.
  - Concept and feasibility studies for new diagnostic methods.
- **TRL:** 1-5
- **Keywords:** diagnostic targets, resistance profiles, prototype development, feasibility studies

#### **5200 Research Area / Diagnostics / Development**
- **Focus:** Developing and validating diagnostic tools for clinical and field use.
- **Includes:**
  - Prototype development, technical specification development.
  - Validation of diagnostic accuracy and performance in relevant settings.
- **TRL:** 6-8
- **Keywords:** diagnostic validation, prototype development, technical specifications, accuracy

#### **5400 Research Area / Diagnostics / Approval & Post approval**
- **Focus:** Regulatory approval and post-market activities for diagnostic tools.
- **Includes:**
  - Submission of diagnostic tools for regulatory approval.
  - Post-approval monitoring and performance optimization.
- **TRL:** 9
- **Keywords:** regulatory submission, diagnostic approval, post-market monitoring, optimization

### **1.6 Promotants**

#### **5600 Research Area / Promotants / Promotants**
- **Focus:** Products that enhance health or productivity without addressing disease.
- **Includes:**
  - Probiotics, prebiotics, antimicrobial peptides, phytochemicals.
  - Organic acids, enzymes, and other growth-promoting alternatives.
- **TRL:** Varies based on specific promotant.
- **Keywords:** probiotics, prebiotics, antimicrobial peptides, phytochemicals, growth-promoting

### **1.7 Other Products**

#### **5500 Research Area / Other Products / Other Products**
- **Focus:** External or topical applications not classified under other categories.
- **Includes:**
  - Biocides, antiseptics, disinfectants.
  - Biofilm prevention materials, medical devices, wound care products.
  - Health monitoring technologies such as sensors in animals.
- **TRL:** Varies based on product type.
- **Keywords:** biocides, antiseptics, disinfectants, biofilm prevention, medical devices, sensors

### **1.8 Operational Research**

#### **6100 Research Area / Operational / Operational**
- **Focus:** Research supporting decision-making and management strategies in AMR.
- **Includes:**
  - Infection prevention and control practices.
  - Antimicrobial stewardship, access and availability studies.
  - Surveillance, epidemiological studies, social science research on behavior change, economic analyses.
- **TRL:** N/A (Operational research does not fit into TRLs)
- **Keywords:** infection prevention, antimicrobial stewardship, surveillance, epidemiological studies, economic analyses

### **1.9 Capacity Building**

#### **7200 Research Area / Capacity Building / Capacity Building**
- **Focus:** Enhancing human and infrastructural resources to tackle AMR.
- **Includes:**
  - Laboratory capacity enhancement, training, and staff development.
  - Network formation for knowledge sharing.
  - Process improvements beyond single products.
- **TRL:** N/A
- **Keywords:** laboratory capacity, training, staff development, knowledge sharing, process improvements

### **1.10 Policy**

#### **7100 Research Area / Policy / Policy**
- **Focus:** Informing policy and regulation development or revision related to AMR.
- **Includes:**
  - Research proposing policy changes or stakeholder actions.
  - Studies on healthcare services impact, economic models, health technology assessments.
- **TRL:** N/A
- **Keywords:** policy development, regulation, economic models, health technology assessments

## **3. Guidelines for Accurate Classification**

### **3.1 Distinguishing Therapeutics Categories**

- **Therapeutics/Discovery (3100):**
  - **Focus:** Identifying new drug targets or active compounds.
  - **Indicators:** "target validation," "hit identification," "lead optimization."
  
- **Therapeutics/Development (3200):**
  - **Focus:** Advancing therapeutics through clinical trials.
  - **Indicators:** "clinical trials," "Phase 1-3," "efficacy testing," "safety studies."
  
- **Therapeutics/Approval & Post approval (3400):**
  - **Focus:** Regulatory approval and post-market activities.
  - **Indicators:** "regulatory submission," "NDA," "pharmacovigilance," "market surveillance."

### **3.2 Avoiding Over-Classification into Development**

- **Contextual Alignment:**
  - Determine if the research is about identifying new therapeutics (**Discovery**) vs. advancing known candidates (**Development**).
  
- **Specific Indicators:**
  - **Development:** Terms like "clinical trials," "Phase 1-3."
  - **Discovery:** Terms like "target identification," "lead optimization."
  
- **Basic Research Overlap:**
  - If focused on understanding disease mechanisms without direct product application, classify under **Basic Research** instead of **Therapeutics**.

### **3.3 Leveraging TRLs Effectively**

- **Map Activities to TRLs:**
  - Align the research stage with the appropriate TRL range.
  
- **Ensure Activity Fit:**
  - Verify that described activities correspond to the TRL of the target category.

### **3.4 Comprehensive Category Definitions**

- **Avoid Ambiguity:**
  - Clearly define each category with specific activities and focus areas to minimize overlap.
  
- **Mutually Exclusive Classifications:**
  - Ensure each classification is distinct based on refined definitions.

## **4. Examples for Clarification**

### **4.1 Correct Classification Examples**

- **Discovery:**
  - *"Identifying novel inhibitors targeting bacterial resistance mechanisms."* → **3100 Research Area / Therapeutics / Discovery**
  
- **Development:**
  - *"Conducting Phase 2 clinical trials for a new antibiotic."* → **3202 Research Area / Therapeutics / Development / Phase 2**
  
- **Basic Research:**
  - *"Studying the genetic basis of pathogen virulence in vitro."* → **2100 Research Area / Basic Research / Fundamental**
  
- **Policy:**
  - *"Assessing the economic impact of antimicrobial stewardship programs."* → **7100 Research Area / Policy / Policy**

### **4.2 Avoiding Misclassification**

- **Misclassified as Development:**
  - *"Exploring the role of microbiomes in disease resistance."* → Should be **2100 Research Area / Basic Research / Fundamental**, not **3200 Therapeutics/Development**.
  
- **Misclassified as Therapeutics/Development:**
  - *"Developing models to predict vaccine efficacy."* → Should be **4110 Research Area / Vaccines / Discovery** or **4120 Research Area / Vaccines / Development**, depending on the stage.



Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

TRL 1: Basic principles observed.
TRL 2: Concept formulated.
TRL 3: Experimental proof of concept.
TRL 4: Technology validated in the lab.
TRL 5: Technology validated in a relevant environment.
TRL 6: Technology demonstrated in a relevant environment.
TRL 7: System prototype in an operational environment.
TRL 8: System complete and qualified.
TRL 9: Actual system proven in operational environment.
