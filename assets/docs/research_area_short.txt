Research Areas and Subcategories

	1.	Basic Research
	•	Fundamental research aims to understand diseases, immune responses, or pathogens (TRL 1-3).
	•	Fundamental: Explores mechanisms like transmission, virulence, and immunology without a clear product path (TRL 1-2).
	•	Towards a product: Focuses on identifying therapeutic, preventive, or diagnostic targets and enabling technologies (TRL 3).
	2.	Therapeutics
	•	Develops products for treating infections, including antimicrobials, alternatives, and combination therapies.
	•	Discovery: Involves target validation, hit discovery, and preclinical testing of new treatments.
	•	Clinical Testing: Conducts clinical trials (Phases 1-3) to evaluate safety and efficacy.
	•	Approval/Post-Approval: Covers product commercialization, post-market studies, and optimization.
	3.	Vaccines
	•	Develops products stimulating the adaptive immune system to prevent disease.
	•	Discovery: Identifies and improves vaccine components like antigens and adjuvants.
	•	Clinical Testing: Conducts trials (Phases 1-3) to assess safety and effectiveness.
	•	Approval/Post-Approval: Manages regulatory filings, post-approval studies, and product optimization.
	4.	Preventives - Other
	•	Focuses on non-vaccine products preventing diseases, such as immune modulators and anti-biofilm agents.
	•	Discovery: Identifies and characterizes preventive products.
	•	Clinical Testing: Validates products through trials (Phases 1-3).
	•	Approval/Post-Approval: Handles regulatory approval, monitoring, and optimization.
	5.	Diagnostics
	•	Develops tools for detecting, screening, or diagnosing diseases and resistance profiles.
	•	Discovery: Focuses on prototype development and feasibility studies.
	•	Clinical Testing: Validates performance in trials and routine settings.
	•	Approval/Post-Approval: Involves regulatory filings, monitoring, and optimization.
	6.	Promotants
	•	Develops products improving health or productivity, such as probiotics, prebiotics, and growth promoters.
	7.	Other Products
	•	Focuses on external or topical applications, including biocides, biofilm prevention, and medical devices.
	8.	Operational Research
	•	Improves decision-making through studies on infection control, stewardship, and access to AMR technologies.
	9.	Capacity Building
	•	Enhances infrastructure, training, and networks to tackle antimicrobial resistance.
	10.	Policy

	•	Informs policies through research on healthcare impacts, economic models, and regulations.

Product Development Stages

	1.	Discovery
	•	Focuses on validating targets, developing prototypes, and conducting preclinical trials (TRL 4-5).
	2.	Clinical Testing
	•	Evaluates products’ safety and effectiveness through trials (Phases 1-3) and manufacturing validation (TRL 6-8).
	3.	Approval and Post-Approval
	•	Manages regulatory filings, monitoring, and optimization of approved products (TRL 9).

Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

	•	TRL 1-3: Observes principles, formulates concepts, and validates them experimentally.
	•	TRL 4-6: Validates and demonstrates technologies in lab or relevant environments.
	•	TRL 7-9: Develops prototypes, qualifies systems, and proves them operationally.
