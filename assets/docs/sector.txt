Sector


Animal Sector

Focus: Classification of animals based on their usage, characteristics, and relationship to humans.

Sub-Categories:

Livestock
Cattle: Includes dairy, beef, and meat-producing cattle (cows, bulls, oxen, calves).
Small Ruminants: Refers to sheep (Ovis spp) and goats (Capra spp).
Pig: Includes domesticated pigs (genus Sus), covering swine, hogs, and pork.
Livestock - Other Food: Encompasses domesticated or farmed wild animals raised for food, such as buffalo, yaks, camels, horses, mules, rabbits, etc.
Livestock - Other Non-Food: Includes animals used for labor or other non-food products, such as skin and fur.
Not Specified: Refers to general terms like “livestock” without specific identification.

Poultry
Chicken: Refers specifically to chickens (Gallus domesticus), including hens, roosters, chicks, and broilers.
Other Poultry: Covers other domesticated birds like turkeys, quails, ostriches, pigeons, and ducks.
Not Specified: General term “poultry” without additional information.

Aquaculture
Fish: Fresh or saltwater species such as carp, salmon, tilapia, and catfish.
Other Aquaculture: Includes other aquatic species like crustaceans, mollusks, and amphibians.
Not Specified: General term “aquaculture” without further specification.

Insects
Insects: Small hexapod invertebrates, including domesticated insects such as honeybees (Apis) and silkworms.

Companion
Companion: Animals kept as pets or used in medical/educational settings, including cats, dogs, ferrets, rodents, birds, and some reptiles.


Wildlife
Wildlife: Non-domesticated animals living independently of human control, including both native and non-native species.
Plant Sector

Focus: Classification of plants based on usage and plant parts, including crops, fruits, vegetables, oil seeds, and wild plants.

Sub-Categories:

Cereals
Cereals: Grasses cultivated for edible grains, such as wheat, rice, maize, barley, oats, ancient grains (e.g., quinoa, sorghum, millet).
Crop
Crop: Plants cultivated on a large scale without specific identification, e.g., fiber crops (cotton, flax), industrial crops, and animal feed.
Fruits
Berries: Small fruits eaten whole, such as strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, and blackberries. Commonly known berries that are botanically not true berries are included.
Citrus: Fruits from the Rutaceae family, including oranges, lemons, limes, and grapefruits.
Stone: Fruits with a pit or “stone” inside, like plums, cherries, apricots, and peaches.
Not Specified: General term “fruits” without further specification.
Other: Includes fruits not covered in the other sub-categories, such as bananas, apples, pears, and avocados.

Oil Seeds
Oil Seeds: Grains grown primarily for oil extraction, like sunflower, soybean, canola, and sesame seeds.

Pulses/Beans
Pulses/Beans: Legumes harvested for consumption, like beans (kidney, pinto, black), chickpeas, and lentils. Excludes green legumes like peas that are classified as vegetables.

Tree Nuts
Tree Nuts: Nuts growing on trees, such as hazelnuts, chestnuts, walnuts, pecans, and almonds.
Vegetables
Fruit: Botanically fruits but classified as vegetables due to culinary use, e.g., tomatoes, bell peppers, cucumbers, eggplants.
Leafy Greens: Edible leaves of plants, like lettuce, spinach, kale, and collards.
Roots/Tubers: Underground parts consumed as food, like potatoes, carrots, yams, and onions.
Stems/Flowers: Flowering parts eaten as vegetables, such as broccoli, asparagus, celery, and rhubarb.
Mushrooms: Fungi eaten as vegetables, like button mushrooms and shiitake.
Not Specified: General term “vegetables” without further specification.
Other: Vegetables not covered by the above sub-categories.

Wild Plants
Wild Plants: Native or indigenous plants not cultivated by humans, e.g., ash trees, European conifer forests.

Plant - Not Specified
Plant - Not Specified: Plants without additional information on species or group.
Plant - Other
Plant - Other: Plants not classified in other categories, including medicinal plants, herbs, and food additives (e.g., spices, condiments).

Environment Sector

Focus: Classification of environmental components relevant to research and development, particularly in the context of antimicrobial resistance (AMR).

Sub-Categories:

Wastewater
Health Facilities: Wastewater originating from healthcare settings such as hospitals, nursing homes, and medical labs.
Agriculture: Wastewater from animal and plant production activities, including drainage water.
Aquaculture: Wastewater from farming of aquatic animals.
Industrial: Wastewater from industrial activities, including antimicrobial manufacturing.
Domestic: Wastewater from households and communities, excluding industrial wastewater.
Not Specified: General term “wastewater” without further details.
Other: Any wastewater not covered by other categories, like stormwater or treatment plant wastewater.

Water
Water-Surface: Water in natural bodies, such as rivers, lakes, oceans, and streams.
Water-Not Specified: General term “water” without further information.
Water-Other: Specific types of water like drinking water, irrigation, recreational water, and reclaimed water.

Soil-Waste
Compost: Organic matter from plant origin and green manure.
Manure: Organic matter from animals used in agriculture, such as animal bedding.
Sludge: Material from wastewater treatment, often from industrial processes.
Not Specified: General term “soil-waste” without further details.
Other: Any soil-waste not covered by other categories.

Soil
Soil: Any type of soil with no additional details provided, applicable to agricultural, forest, or grassland soils.
Air
Air: Gaseous substances in the atmosphere, potentially including dust.

Soil
Soil: Any type of soil with no additional details provided, applicable to agricultural, forest, or grassland soils.

Environment-Not Specified
Environment-Not Specified: General term “environment” without specific information.

Environment-Other
Environment-Other: Any environmental aspect not covered by other categories.
